Visitor Guidelines
  • Footwear: Please wear comfortable, closed-toe walking shoes suitable for unpaved farm trails.
  • Zero Single-Use Plastic: We are a zero-waste sanctuary. Bring your reusable canteen; clean spring water is provided.
  • Animal Care: Ducks and ducklings roam freely. Gentle voices and slow footsteps keep them calm.
