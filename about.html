Born from Living Soil

The Rhythm of Nature,
The Frequency of Life.

How a passion for chemical-free soil, ethical pasture management, and ancient handmade crafts blossomed into Purreins Frequency.

Our Foundation

Reclaiming What Industrial Agriculture Forgot

Modern mass production severed the sacred connection between human wellness and the soil beneath our feet. Vegetables stripped of minerals, soaps loaded with synthetic detergents, and grains bleached of life.

We founded Purreins Frequency to prove there is another way: where ducks graze freely along wetland reeds, whole herbs thrive on rich natural compost, and handcrafted curd soap cured patiently over 6 weeks provides pure nourishment to human skin.

Our Resources

Harvested from Tamizhnad • Ilavam Panju

Ilavam Panju (Silk Cotton) — Harvested from Tamizhnad

Our raw Ilavam Panju (silk cotton) is harvested from Tamizhnad directly from natural silk cotton pods. It fills our heirloom quilts with buoyant, breathable warmth—paired with a handcrafted weave of organic cotton thread yarns, handcrafted tote carry bags, and 100% eco-friendly labels with NO PLASTIC.

Certified Organic Fabric • Eco-Friendly Labels • NO PLASTIC

Crafted from GOTS & OEKO-TEX® Certified Fabric

Clarification: Our handcrafted quilts and throws themselves are not what are certified—it is the organic fabric they are made from that is GOTS certified and OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 certified. Woven from organic cotton thread yarns, organically dyed, finished with all eco-friendly labels (NO PLASTIC), and paired with handcrafted tote carry bags.

100% Natural Soil Fertility • Zero Chemicals No Chemical Fertilization

No Chemical Fertilization — Duck, Geese & Puppy Poop, Excess Food & Firewood Ash

Zero chemical fertilization ever touches our soil. Every single plant and tree on our farm is nourished exclusively by our own natural farmstead fertilizers: duck, geese, and puppy poop, excess food, firewood ash, and mulched sun-dried leaves.

Soap Alchemy

Fresh Coconut Oil, Farm Curd & Infusions

Crafted strictly with fresh cold-pressed coconut oil, fresh farm-made curd, and farm-grown infusions for the soaps—zero other oils, zero synthetics.

Botanical Harvest

Sun-Dried & Farm-Grown Leaves

Whole sun-dried leaves and fresh harvests nourished by duck, geese, and puppy poop, excess food, and firewood ash: farm-grown banana leaves, moringa, papaya leaves, tulasi, curry leaves, and more.

Farmstead Guardians

Dalmatian Farm Guard Puppies

Our Dalmatian farm guard puppies faithfully protect the duck chicks, the white geese and goslings, and the entire farm—while their puppy poop enriches our living soil.

Zero Plastic Standard

All Eco-Friendly Labels — NO PLASTIC

Our labels are all eco-friendly too — strictly NO PLASTIC. Every soap wrap, cotton twine tie, herbal pouch, label, and handcrafted tote carry bag is 100% plastic-free and biodegradable.

Slide 01 • White Pekin Ducks • 12×4 Ft Tiled Pool

Ducks Swimming in an Organic Pool

Our free-range White Pekin ducks spend their days swimming and splashing in a clean 12×4 feet tiled organic pool, set right into the earth with lush natural grass and greenery thriving all around it—with zero plastic anywhere.

The nutrient-rich pool water and duck droppings cycle straight back into our soil—powering our banana, moringa, papaya, tulasi, and curry leaf trees with zero chemical fertilizers.

Slide 02 • True Free-Ranging Life

Free-Ranging Ducks & Cheerful Chicks

No cages, no cramped sheds. Our free-ranging White Pekin ducks and cheerful duck and geese chicks run, play, and forage freely across open grass under natural sunlight—growing strong on fresh air, meadow greens, and their own natural rhythm.

Watching the duck chicks and geese chicks play in the grass shows what real free-ranging freedom looks like from day one.

Slide 03 • Protective Geese Sanctuary

White Geese & Their Chicks in the Dry Grass & Coir Hut

Our white geese are fiercely devoted parents—they never like to leave their chicks alone around the White Pekin ducks. We built them their own sunlit wooden shelter bedded generously with sun-cured dry grass and raw coconut coir.

Inside this warm wooden dry-grass and coir sanctuary out in the sun, the white geese sit peacefully with their chicks tucked safely beside them.

Slide 04 • Farmstead Protectors

Dalmatian Farm Guard Puppies Protecting the Chicks, Geese & Farm

Our Dalmatian farm guard puppies are raised right alongside the flock—standing watch over the White Pekin ducks, the cheerful duck chicks, the white geese and goslings, and the entire farmstead across the open grass.

Along with protecting the sanctuary, their puppy poop joins our duck and geese poop, excess food, and firewood ash to nourish our chemical-free soil.
