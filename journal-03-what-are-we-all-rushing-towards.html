What’re we all rushing towards?

Dispatches from the forest • Purreins Frequency

AI is good - Assistance is good - Speed is good.

But none of that can equal nature, the process of nature, the sequence of nature.

“The only way to get healthy, is to slow down. Not shut down, just slow down, take a moment, question yourself, run through your goals, prioritise them, realise them, balance them, with your self existence.”

Yes, achieving is important, but you need to be there to achieve it. You are as much important as the goals you have. And the way to do is the be alive, be healthy, be active.

✦ There is a race, one that we made for ourselves, nothing that nature made against us, except the food chain (which some of us aren’t even aware of).
✦ To be part of the human race, you need to be aligned with the nature’s race at the least.

There’s no time, you might say, yes, there is no time, for yourself, you are giving away all your time to the race and others in the race. The rushing will stop when you get this context.
