Pasture Roaming — Pure Straw Nesting
Ethical Farmstead Habitat

White Pekin Ducks & Their Straw Sanctuary

Meet our gentle white Pekin family. Roaming freely along natural wetland banks and pecking through clean golden straw and coir bedding, they roost in their hand-built wooden shelter.

Unhurried, free-range living yields richer, deeply nourishing pasture eggs with golden yolks—untouched by cages, artificial hormones, or synthetic feeds.
